## 4. Cookies

The Service uses cookies for the following purposes:

- **Essential functions**: keeping you logged in and remembering your preferences, such as language and appearance.
- **Security**: Cloudflare uses cookies to identify malicious traffic and bots in order to protect the Service.
- **Analytics**: Cloudflare and Google Analytics collect information about how the website is used, helping us understand and improve the Service.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, but some features (such as logging in) may not work properly as a result.

- Request a copy of your personal data.

- Request that we stop collecting, processing, or using your personal data.
- Request deletion.
